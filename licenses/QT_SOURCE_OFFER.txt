BDSubMerge Windows artifacts bundle the LGPL-licensed Qt libraries supplied by
the PySide6 wheel as separate files under _internal/PySide6/Qt. They are not
statically linked into BDSubMerge.exe and may be replaced by the user with a
compatible build of the same Qt version.

The corresponding Qt for Python and Qt source archives for version 6.11.1 are
available from the official Qt download service:

https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/pyside6/PySide6-6.11.1-src/
https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/6.11/6.11.1/submodules/

Qt for Python license information:

https://doc.qt.io/qtforpython-6/licenses.html
